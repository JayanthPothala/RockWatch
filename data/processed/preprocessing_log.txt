DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS & SCIENTIFIC DISCLOSURES
================================================================================
1. Slope_z Quantisation:
   - Slope_z was computed by the original authors from integer-degree slope (0°-76°).
   - This produces exactly 76 discrete quantisation bins (~0.5° resolution).
2. Spatial Independence Limitation:
   - The 500 m boundary exclusion zone prevents immediate cross-boundary pixel leakage.
   - However, regional environmental variables (NDVI, rainfall, elevation) possess spatial
     autocorrelation lengths spanning 5-50 km; this boundary buffer is a precaution, not
     proof of complete spatial independence.
3. Longitude Distribution Imbalance:
   - The held-out test split is geographically located in northern HP (predominantly Chamba).
   - Eastern high-altitude regions (Kinnaur, Spiti at 77°-79°E) are not represented in test.
4. Pseudo-Absence Uncertainty:
   - Class=0 locations are randomly sampled pseudo-absences from GEE, not confirmed stable ground.
